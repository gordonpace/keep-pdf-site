Keep PDF

A personal Chrome extension by Gordon Pace that lets you attach PDFs to existing text notes on Google Keep.

Open a note and drag a PDF onto it. Keep PDF uploads the file to a dedicated Keep attachments folder in your Google Drive and adds its filename and Drive link to the note. With the extension installed, the attachment appears as a paperclip followed by a clickable filename.

Your files stay in your account

PDFs go directly from Chrome to Google Drive. The extension uses Google's drive.file permission to work with files it creates or is explicitly granted access to. It does not request access to your entire Drive.

Keep PDF does not make files public or change their sharing permissions. Sharing a note does not automatically share its PDFs. There are no ads, analytics or developer-operated servers receiving your notes or files.

Availability

Keep PDF is currently a personal, unpacked Chrome extension. This website describes the app and its privacy practices; it does not offer a Chrome Web Store installation.

Keep PDF is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google. Changes to Google Keep may require extension updates.
