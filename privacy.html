Privacy policy

Effective date: 3 October 2026. This policy covers the Keep PDF Chrome extension maintained by Gordon Pace.

Data used by the extension

The extension processes PDFs you deliberately drop onto an open Google Keep text note, including their contents, filenames and sizes. It reads relevant note text and page elements locally to identify the target note, insert an attachment link and display existing attachments.

After you authorize Google Drive access, the extension retrieves your Drive account email address and account identifier to display the connected account and keep upload records associated with the correct account. Chrome's identity service supplies Google OAuth access tokens, which the extension uses to authenticate requests to Google Drive.

Where data goes

PDF contents and filenames are sent directly to Google's Drive API and saved in the Keep attachments folder in your account. The filename and Drive URL are inserted into the selected Keep note and saved through Google Keep's normal service. OAuth tokens are used for authenticated requests to Google.

The extension does not send notes, PDFs, account information or credentials to the developer or any developer-operated server. It does not sell data, use advertising or analytics, or use your data to train AI models.

Permissions and local storage

The Google OAuth scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file allows access to files created by this app or explicitly made available to it. The extension does not request access to your entire Google Drive.

Chrome local extension storage holds the connected account email and identifier, the attachment folder identifier, and upload metadata such as filenames, file identifiers, sizes, Drive URLs, timestamps and completion status. These records support recent-upload display and recovery. The extension does not persist PDF contents in this local storage.

Sharing, retention and deletion

The extension does not change Drive sharing permissions. Access to files follows your Drive account and folder settings. If you share a Keep note, its recipients may see filenames and links; share the corresponding Drive files separately if you want them to have access.

PDFs and note links remain in your Google account until you remove them. Removing an attachment link or deleting a note does not delete its PDF. Delete PDFs in Google Drive, and remove attachment text in Google Keep, to remove those copies. Uninstalling the extension removes its local extension storage but does not remove your Drive files or Keep notes. You can revoke the app's Google access in your Google Account's third-party connections settings.

This website

These static pages are hosted by GitHub Pages and contain no added analytics, tracking scripts or forms. GitHub may process technical request information as described in its privacy statement. Google's handling of Drive, Keep and authorization data is governed by Google's privacy policy.
